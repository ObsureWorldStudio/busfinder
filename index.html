How it works

No more guessing whether the bus has passed. Riders help each other, a bit like a group chat for the bus stop.

1

Find your route

Search any A Re Yeng or Tshwane bus route, see it on the map, and open its official timetable.

2

On a bus? Tap "I'm on the bus"

Your phone shares the bus's position while you ride, until you tap "I got off". Nobody sees who you are.

3

Everyone sees the bus

People waiting further down the route see the bus moving. After you get off, it stays on the map as "last seen".

How to install it

  1. Tap Download Bus Finder. If Chrome asks whether to keep the file, tap Download anyway.
  2. When it's done, tap Open (or find bus-finder.apk in your Downloads).
  3. If your phone says it can't install apps from this source, tap Settings, switch on Allow from this source, then go back.
  4. Tap Install. If Google Play Protect says the app is unknown, tap More details, then Install anyway. It says this because the app isn't on Google Play yet.
  5. Open Bus Finder from your apps.

Only download Bus Finder from this page. When it arrives on Google Play, you may need to uninstall this version before installing the Play one.
