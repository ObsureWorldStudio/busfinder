Bus Finder

Privacy Policy

Effective 15 October 2026 · Written in plain language

The short version

  • You don't need an account. We never ask for your name, phone number, email or ID number.
  • Your location is only used when you tap "I'm on the bus", and only until you tap "I got off" (or after 90 minutes).
  • The map shows buses, never people. Nobody can see who shared a bus.
  • Shared bus positions are deleted automatically within 30 minutes.
  • We don't sell your information, and we never use your location for advertising.

1. Who we are

Bus Finder ("the app", "we", "us") is an independent app that shows where buses in the City of Tshwane are, based on positions shared by people riding them. 2. What information the app uses, and why

When you just look at the map

We don't collect personal information. To show the map and the buses, your phone connects to our database (Supabase) and to the OpenStreetMap map servers. Like any website, these servers can see your phone's internet (IP) address and app version while they send you the data. We don't store these addresses ourselves, and we don't use them to identify you.

If you tap My location, your position is used only on your phone to show the blue dot. It is not sent to us.

When you tap "I'm on the bus"

While you are sharing, the app sends the following to our database about every 10 seconds:

WhatWhy
Your phone's GPS position (latitude and longitude)To show where the bus is
Direction and speed of travelTo show whether the bus is moving
The route you picked (e.g. "T1")To show the bus on the right route
A random trip codeTo update one bus dot instead of creating a new one every 10 seconds. A new code is made for every trip, so trips can't be linked to each other or to you.
Times (when sharing started and the last update)To show how fresh the position is and to delete old positions

We do not collect your name, phone number, email, contacts, photos, device ID or advertising ID.

Other app users see a bus dot on the route you chose, with how long ago it was updated and roughly how fast it is moving. They never see the trip code or anything about you.

Location in the background

After you tap "I'm on the bus", sharing keeps working when your screen is off or you switch apps, so the bus stays on the map during your ride. Android shows a notification the whole time ("Bus Finder: sharing your bus"). Sharing stops when you tap "I got off", after 90 minutes, or if you leave the Tshwane area. The app only asks for location permission while using the app. It never asks for "Allow all the time".

Stored only on your phone

The app saves a few things on your own phone: that you've seen the welcome screen and the location explanation, and a copy of the route lines (so you don't use mobile data downloading them every time). Uninstalling the app removes all of this.

If you email us

We use your email address and message only to answer you, and delete them within 12 months.

3. How long we keep it

When you tap "I got off", or if your phone stops sending positions, the bus stays on the map as "last seen" so other riders know roughly where it was. Every shared position is deleted automatically after 30 minutes. We keep no history of trips.

Our database host keeps short technical logs (for example IP addresses of requests) for security and fault-finding, for no more than a few days.

4. Who processes it, and where

  • Supabase: hosts our database. Servers in London, United Kingdom.
  • OpenStreetMap Foundation: provides the map pictures. Servers in Europe.
  • Google: delivers the app through Google Play, and Android provides the GPS position, under Google's own privacy policy.

Because these servers are outside South Africa, information is transferred across borders. We only use providers in countries with data-protection laws that give protection similar to POPIA (section 72), such as the United Kingdom's data-protection law.

We do not sell, rent or trade any information. We would only share information if the law requires it, and because positions are deleted within 30 minutes, we usually have nothing to share.

5. Advertising and sponsors

The app may show a sponsor message (for example a local business). Sponsor messages are plain text and links. They don't collect or receive any of your information. Your location is never used for advertising. If we ever add an advertising service, we will update this policy first and tell you in the app.

6. Children

Anyone may look at the map. Sharing a bus's location is for people aged 18 and older, and the app asks you to confirm this before sharing starts. We don't knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has shared through the app, contact us.

7. Keeping it safe

All information travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). The database only accepts positions through checked functions that reject impossible or out-of-area positions, and nobody using the app can read the stored trip codes. Very little is stored, for a very short time, which is the best protection of all.

8. Your rights under POPIA

You have the right to know what personal information we hold about you, to ask us to correct or delete it, and to object to it being used. Because we don't ask who you are and positions are deleted within 30 minutes, we usually cannot link any stored information to you. But please contact us and we will help.

You are always in control: you can stop sharing at any time by tapping "I got off", turn off location for Bus Finder in your phone's settings, or uninstall the app.
